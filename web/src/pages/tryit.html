Try it in your browser

Everything on this page runs locally: the record is made or read in your browser and classified by the gridmon C core compiled to WebAssembly (the same C99 source as the PC tests and the C2000 build). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

The classifier was built for 1 s records at 5 kHz in which the event starts 0.2–0.4 s into the record and the first 0.15 s is undisturbed. Records that do not follow this (an event at the very start, or two events) are outside what it was designed for.

A. Synthesise an event

A simplified port of the project's phasor-domain generator (sequence phasors, Bollen dip types, a frequency ramp, a modulated envelope; harmonics and noise; 12-bit quantisation). It is a demonstration, not the dataset generator: no 20 kHz synthesis chain, no anti-alias filter, fixed nuisances.

B. Upload a CSV

Three voltage columns (optionally preceded by a time column in seconds), one row per sample, a header line is allowed. Give the channel type, the nominal line-to-line RMS voltage and (without a time column) the sample rate. The page converts to per unit, forms line-to-line voltages from line-to-neutral if needed, resamples to 5 kHz with a windowed-sinc filter (Blackman window, cut-off 0.45 × the lower of the two rates, gain normalised to 1), and lets you choose the 1 s window to classify. Examples: unbalanced sag, LN, 10 kHz · phase jump, LL, 5 kHz (both 400 V).
